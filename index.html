About KPATEI

From Creative Vision
to Physical Execution.

KPATEI CONCEPT & BUILD is a premier architectural drafting, technical design, construction supervision and interior design studio based in Freetown, Sierra Leone. We bridge the gap between creative vision and physical execution, offering complete end-to-end building solutions.

Whether you live in Sierra Leone or abroad as part of the diaspora, we take full control of your project, making sure every residential or commercial building is executed to the highest standards of safety, quality and precision.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate technical documentation, flawless construction execution and stunning interior spaces, providing total peace of mind to our clients through transparent daily communication and professional craftsmanship.
