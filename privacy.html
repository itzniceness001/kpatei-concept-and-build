Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information KPATEI Concept & Build ("we", "us") collects when you use our website www.kpateiconceptandbuild.com and our Android app, which shows the same website. It also explains how we use it and the choices you have.

In short: we only collect what you choose to send us, we use it to answer you and to work on your project, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, phone number, the type of plan you are interested in and your message. The form is delivered to our email by the service Web3Forms.
  • Request a Project form: your name, phone number, building type, rooms, floors, plot size, location, the services you need and your message. This form does not send anything to us by itself: it opens WhatsApp on your device with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent there.
  • Client reviews: your name, the area you live in, the service we did for you, your star rating and your review. Reviews you post are shown publicly on our website. They are stored in a Google Sheet owned by us. Reviews containing abusive words, links or phone numbers are rejected automatically.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email you send us directly.

Information collected automatically

We do not use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics tools. Like every website, our pages load fonts, icons and styling from outside services (Google Fonts, cdnjs and the Tailwind CSS service). These services receive your device's internet (IP) address and basic browser information so they can deliver those files. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may also keep standard technical logs.

To make the app open quickly and work without internet, a copy of our pages and pictures is saved on your own device. This copy contains no personal information.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and give you prices
  • To prepare your drawings and carry out the work you ask us to do, including construction supervision and site updates
  • To show client reviews on our website

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the services listed above that help us run the website, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and project details for as long as we need them to work with you and to keep proper business records. Reviews stay on our website until you ask us to remove them or we remove them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to remove your review from our website. Contact us using the details below and we will do so.

Children

Our website and app are for adults planning building projects. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Staff Clock-In tool

Our website also hosts a separate staff attendance tool for KPATEI employees. Its staff records and photos are stored only on the company device that runs it and are not sent to our website.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
